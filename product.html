Live reporting · Start here

SAMePOS Dashboard

Your whole venue, live, from anywhere.

What it does

Live sales from every till, on your phone or laptop. It updates every minute, so you can check in from home, the airport or the next venue.

It runs beside the till system you already have (DigitotPOS today), so nothing gets ripped out.

Key benefits

  • Sales today, orders, average order and open tabs at a glance.
  • Sales by hour, voids and deletes, and cash variance for the day.
  • Alerts by severity, so you know what to look at first.
  • Print or save any page as a PDF.

Alerts, live today

  • A cash-up out by more than R50.
  • A till quiet for 6 hours while the day is open.
  • A day left unclosed.
  • A licence about to run out.
  • Loss flags, such as an item voided after payment.

Coming soon Sales-vs-stock detection.

Front of house

SAMePOS Tills

Sell fast. Even when the internet drops.

What it does

The touch selling screen your staff use at the bar and on the floor. Open tabs, ring up orders and take payment. Stock comes off the count the moment a sale is paid.

Key benefits

  • Keeps selling offline, then catches up on its own.
  • Faster service: big buttons, an open-tab switcher and one tap to Pay.
  • Fewer mistakes: voids, discounts and refunds need a manager PIN.

Stock and paperwork

SAMePOS Back Office

Know what you bought, what you sold, and what's on the shelf.

What it does

Where managers run the stock side of the venue: suppliers, purchase orders, goods received and stocktakes, plus quotes, invoices, credit notes and payments.

Key benefits

  • Buy smarter: purchase orders and goods received in one place, with a credit note when a delivery comes up short.
  • Clean records: invoices, credit notes and payments that match the till.
  • Stock you can trust: stock on hand and stocktakes, with every report ready to print or export.

Accounting

SAMeSync

Your books match your till. Nobody retypes a sale.

  1. SAMePOS tills
  2. SAMeSync
  3. QuickBooks, or a daily file for your accountant

What it does

Sends each closed trading day from SAMePOS to your accounting software.

Key benefits

  • QuickBooks first, plus a daily file your accountant can import into any package.
  • VAT totals ready for your accountant.
  • Less admin and fewer errors: no manual capture, no missing days.
