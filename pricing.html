Pricing questions

How is SAMePOS licensed?

Through recurring monthly or annual licence fees that fund continuous updates and AI model improvements. Licences are enforced through signed keys, engineered so a lapsed licence never disrupts a live trading session.

Do I have to buy hardware?

No. Scanners, scales and terminals are leased with the system rather than sold, so hardware ownership stays with us and you avoid a large upfront outlay.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. That's the whole idea of the Stock Sidecar. Adopt the AI layer beside your existing POS, then graduate to Full POS Mode when you're ready.

Why don't you list prices?

Because the right plan depends on your venue's size, number of stations and which mode you run. A short conversation lets us quote accurately rather than over- or under-charging you.
