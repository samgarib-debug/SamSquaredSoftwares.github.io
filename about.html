Built from the venue floor up

Sam Squared Softwares makes SAMePOS: software designed around how high-pressure venues actually operate, not how software vendors wish they did.

Our mission

Premium hospitality and retail venues lose margin quietly, through shrinkage, manual cash-ups and hard-to-police voids and comps. We build software that makes those leaks visible in real time, so owners can act while it still matters.

Our approach

SAMePOS was designed from a full reverse-engineering of real incumbent systems and validated against real, messy production data: tens of thousands of audit-log rows and hundreds of thousands of sales records from a live pilot. It's engineered to fit the way venues already work.

What we value

Non-disruptive by design

Licence enforcement can never interrupt a live trading session. You adopt our AI layer without risk to a busy night.

Honest data

Blind count sheets, tamper-evident event logs and self-checking AI. We build for accuracy you can trust and audit.

Always improving

Recurring licences fund continuous updates and AI model improvements, distributed back to every venue over time.
