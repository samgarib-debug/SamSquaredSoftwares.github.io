How SAMePOS works

Adopt the AI layer with zero disruption, then graduate to a full point of sale when you're ready. Two modes, one platform.

Mode 1

Full POS Mode

SAMePOS becomes your point of sale outright, replacing the incumbent system — a fast bartender sales grid, tabs that follow guests between floors, split payments, manager-PIN-gated voids and comps, live mid-event pricing, and a roaming event dashboard.

Mode 2

Standalone Stock Sidecar

Keep your existing POS for sales. SAMePOS reads its nightly export and layers on stock control, variance analysis, cash-up automation and loss prevention — writing results back only through that system's own safe file format. A deliberate low-risk wedge.

The end-to-end flow

From raw venue data to real-time insight, in five steps.

1

Connect

The Echo Engine plugs into your existing POS export — no rip-and-replace. It learns your file's shape once and handles every file after that automatically.

2

Capture

Sales, payments, voids, comps and stock movements become timestamped, tamper-evident events. Supplier invoices are read and posted by the AI pipeline.

3

Detect

Three layers of loss prevention — rules, statistical baselines and learned models — surface issues as Watch, Review or Act alerts, tuned to your venue.

4

Reconcile

Barcode-scan-and-weigh stocktakes and automated per-staff cash-ups reconcile theory against reality — every night, to the cent.

5

Act

Owners get per-staff, per-night visibility on a live dashboard, so margin leaks are caught while they can still be acted on.
