How SAMePOS works

Sold as modules. Start with the Dashboard beside the till you already have, then add the rest when you're ready.

Start here

Start with the Dashboard

Keep the till system you already have (DigitotPOS today). The SAMePOS Dashboard runs beside it and shows live sales from every till, with alerts when something needs a look. Nothing gets ripped out.

When you're ready

Add the rest

Add SAMePOS Tills, the Back Office and SAMeSync when you're ready. The tills keep selling when the internet drops and catch up on their own. Stock comes off the count the moment a sale is paid, and each closed day goes to your books.

The end-to-end flow

From the till to your books, in five steps.

1

Connect

We set up the Dashboard beside your till system. No rip-and-replace, and your staff keep selling the way they do today.

2

Capture

Sales, voids, deletes and cash-ups from every till reach your Dashboard, updated every minute. SAMePOS Tills keep selling offline and catch up when the internet is back.

3

Detect

Alerts by severity flag a cash-up out by more than R50, a till quiet for 6 hours while the day is open, a day left unclosed, a licence about to run out, and loss flags such as an item voided after payment.

4

Reconcile

Close the day. SAMeSync sends it to QuickBooks, or as a daily file your accountant can import into any package, with VAT totals ready.

5

Act

Check in from home, the airport or the next venue, and act on a leak while it still matters.
