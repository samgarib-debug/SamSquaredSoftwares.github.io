2. What we supply

SAMePOS runs in one of two configurations, as recorded on your order:

We may also supply supporting equipment, quoted separately (see section 6), and carry out custom development or integration work under a separate written scope (see section 10).

3.1 Licence enforcement and the trading-hours grace window

Each installation is licensed with a signed key bound to your venue and to a hardware fingerprint of the on-site server. The platform verifies that licence periodically when it has connectivity, and continues to operate through a defined offline grace window when it does not.

We design licence enforcement so that a lapsed or unverifiable licence never interrupts a trading session that is already open. Where enforcement takes effect, it does so by withholding updates and, after the grace window, by preventing new sessions from starting — not by stopping tills mid-service.
